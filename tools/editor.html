    One small file per series, saved as content/series/<series>.md, gives the series its title, description and book credit. Without it the series is still linked, titled from its folder name. Download it once; it is remembered here so later chapters can reuse it.

      2 · Sentences

      One row per sentence, Welsh and English together, so they can never get out of step. Select words in the Welsh (drag or double-click) and press G, or the “Add tooltip” button, to build a tooltip; click a dotted word to edit it. Use Edit (or Enter on a sentence) to change the Welsh text itself. Enter splits a sentence, Alt+↑/↓ moves a row.

        Preview

        Exactly how the article page will render. Hover or tap a dotted word; click a sentence to jump to its row.

        Checks

          Files

            Download the zip, unzip it into content/articles/, then commit and push. Sentence timings are made with the sync tool (load the audio and the downloaded article.md), then put timings.json in the same folder, or open the folder here to keep it in the zip.
