Countries in Europe build big wind turbines in the sea for clean energy from wind.
But millions of birds fly over the sea every year.
People want to keep them safe.

Scientists put sixteen thermal cameras on a turbine to watch birds during the day and night.
In one year, millions of birds fly near the turbine.
Only two birds hit it.
Other studies also show that almost all birds fly around the turbines safely.

Experts say that these results are good news.
But we must still help the birds and keep them safe.
